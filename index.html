About Us

J.K. Enterprises is a Mumbai-based pioneer manufacturer and turnkey service provider specializing in advanced automated and mechanical vehicle parking infrastructure. We expertly design, construct, and implement modern vehicle stacking arrangements configured to generate maximum parking yields within highly tight dimensional boundaries.


Led by CEO Nilesh Mantri, our firm delivers reliable multi-tier pit systems, triple-level storage configurations, high-density rotary variants, and structural cantilever platforms engineered perfectly for contemporary residential societies, premium corporate towers, and industrial spaces.

Our Solutions

Triple Level Parking

Triples your localized vehicle capacity metrics by stacking three independent sedans or SUVs inside a singular structural parking footprint.

Dependent Pit Parking

Sub-grade hydraulic space optimization engineered explicitly for low-clearance residential basements and concrete multi-level setups.

Cantilever Parking

Premium over-hanging structural configurations executing smooth horizontal load transfers without needing ground support pillars on the entry corridor.

Rotary Parking

High-yield automated vertical carousel mechanics engineered to store dozens of vehicles securely within the real estate area of just two standard spaces.
