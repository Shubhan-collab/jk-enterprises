Our Heritage & Legacy

Please allow us to introduce ourselves. We are proudly established in the heavy industrial fabrication landscape, delivering precision-engineered configurations for more than 40 years.

Our parent organization, M/s Allied Electricals and Mechanical Industries, was founded in the year 1969 by Mr. K D Mantri. We commenced operations focusing heavily on the specialized manufacturing of Printing Cylinders, Fixed Shaft Rollers, Rubber Rollers, and Ideal Rollers equipped with Dynamic Balancing systems serving the Flexible Printing industry. Over the years, our core capabilities expanded heavily into structural M.S. / S.S. arrangements and robust precision sheet metal fabrication.

As a recognized pioneer and continuous leader in our domain, our company commands exceptional goodwill and an unblemished structural reputation across commercial markets.

In 2004, we initiated strategic infrastructural expansion by acquiring our second operational manufacturing facility situated at Goregaon East, successfully doubling our net production capacity. Simultaneously, we stepped extensively into technical fabrication frameworks for the HVAC sector—initially delivering specialized Electrical Panel Modules, structural Chiller Platforms, and complex AHU Support layouts.

Progressing naturally from component fabrication, we scaled up into comprehensive turnkey sub-contracts executing Chilled Water Piping and specialized Copper Piping networks for high-capacity PAC Units, VRF Systems, commercial Package units, and advanced industrial ventilation ducting.

Needless to say, we are already catering to the complex technical specifications of diverse large-scale organizations to their entire satisfaction. Today, after successfully commissioning and setting up between 5,000 to 8,000 tons of air conditioning setups on a sub-contract basis, we are scaling forward to manage these large-scale HVAC engineering frameworks directly under our signature corporate umbrella.
